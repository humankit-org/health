Calculator Method & Sources

Sliders in, metrics out — what lifestyle changes are associated with, according to published research.

100% client-side: everything you enter stays in this browser tab. Nothing is stored, sent, or logged — there isn't even a server to send it to.
Rough estimates only — not medical advice, not a prediction about you. This tool replays population-level statistical associations from published epidemiology. They describe averages across hundreds of thousands of people, say nothing certain about cause and effect, and definitely cannot predict an individual's future. Talk to a doctor before changing anything based on a website.
