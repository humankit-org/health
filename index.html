This is a tool that gives stats based on published scientific research, describing averages across thousands of people, and cannot predict your future. Please read the about page. Talk to a doctor as your source of truth, not a website.

What each lever does

Beneath every slider, small chips show what that lever does on its own — per-slider effects, on their own, for the person described by the sliders. These are not added by simply adding them up: factors can overlap (e.g. sitting time and exercise both capture activity), so the model combines them in published joint models instead. A chip reading "counted at 70% — overlaps cardio" means that slider's effect overlaps another, and only a share is counted so you aren't double-charged. Psychosocial factors (stress, purpose, social time, sleep regularity) have no reliable way to be combined yet, so they're shown individually — they never multiply into the mortality / cancer / CVD totals, and on the cognition & wellbeing bands they only nudge their own band, each on its own.
